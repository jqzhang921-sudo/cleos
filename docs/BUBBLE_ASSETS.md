# Bubble decoration provenance

`app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/bubble_sleep.png` and `bubble_bunny.png` were generated with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-07 for Cleos.

The prompts requested original small doodle faces on transparent backgrounds and excluded recognizable licensed characters. They were not extracted from the user's reference screenshots. These are generated raster decorations, not CC0 stock assets; no claim of exclusive rights or blanket legal clearance is made.

Rendering keeps their aspect ratio and uses a fixed 24 dp slot. Background gradients and geometric decorations are drawn separately in `BubbleThemes.kt`.
